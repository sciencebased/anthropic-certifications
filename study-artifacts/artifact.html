The exam's real shape. Four scenarios are drawn from a pool of six, and roughly fifteen questions come from each. It is not sixty independent questions — it is four long cases. The default setting below reproduces that draw.
Two flags, not one. Flag: content when you know what is being asked but not the answer. Flag: wording when the sentence itself is slowing you down. The report separates them so you learn whether your losses are knowledge or reading speed.

    The hinge words

    Stems are long, but the decision usually turns on one construction.

    ConstructionWhat it signals
    regardless of how / independent of / no matter whatThe guarantee must hold when the model is not involved. Rules out prompt-based options; points to deterministic enforcement — a hook, a settings rule, an orchestration check.
    first / initial interventionTests ordering, not correctness. A defensible-but-later step is a deliberate distractor. Ask what comes first, not what would work.
    most directly addressesSeveral options are partially right. Take the one that hits the stated failure at its cause, not the one that adds the most machinery.
    which combination / select twoThe answer must cover every failure listed. Enumerate the symptoms and tick them off against each option.
    before X is verified / until X succeedsA precondition, not a preference. The right control blocks outright rather than asking for confirmation.
    on the reasoning that…You are handed someone's justification and asked to find the flaw in the reasoning — which may differ from the outcome being wrong.
    rather thanNames the anti-pattern under test. Whatever follows it is what the wrong answers will do.
    while keeping … unchangedA constraint, not filler. An option that changes that thing is disqualified however good it sounds.
    has been / continues to / stillPresent perfect marks the fix already tried and failed. Options that do more of it are wrong by construction.
    confirm / logs show / testing revealsEvidence you must treat as true. If logs confirm a subagent returned complete results, the failure is upstream of it.
